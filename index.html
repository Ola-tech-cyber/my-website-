Types of Houses

1. Bungalow

A bungalow is a house that usually has only one main floor. It is often designed with rooms arranged on the same level.

2. Duplex

A duplex is a house that has two main levels or floors. It provides more living space than a typical single-storey house.

3. Skyscraper

A skyscraper is a very tall building with many floors. Skyscrapers are commonly found in large cities and are used for offices, apartments, hotels, and other purposes.

4. Mansion

A mansion is a large and spacious house, usually containing many rooms and facilities. It is generally designed to provide a high level of comfort and living space.
